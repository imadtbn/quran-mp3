# Reciter portraits

`assets/data/reciter-portraits.json` maps MP3Quran **reader IDs** (not recording IDs or display names) to locally hosted WebP portraits. Displayed credits are generated from the same metadata on readers.html.

Before adding a portrait, verify the depicted reciter and the image's reuse terms. Keep its original source URL, author, license, license URL, review date, and `verified: true`. Use a lowercase ASCII filename, resize to at most 240 × 300, and preserve any required attribution and share-alike license for the derivative. Each included derivative retains its source image's license; software licensing does not override it.

Readers without a verified portrait, failed requests, and failed image loads use name initials. Never infer a photograph from a similar name or substitute a generated face.

The initial set includes Mahmoud Khalil al-Hussary (118), Mishary al-Afasy (123), and Abdul Basit Abdul Samad (51). Additional readers remain pending verification. The Abdul Basit source is Prasar Bharati / Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Government of India, 21 March 1980, under GODL-India. Images are resized and converted to WebP; no endorsement by the photographers, reciters, or source institutions is implied.
